## Description — Variant A (concise)  `[~900]`
```
Bestloaders FMS is the carrier app for freight partners who move Bestloaders' loads. Sign in, see what's available, and run every job from your phone.

• Bid on open loads and watch bids update live
• See your assigned loads with full details — route, container, rate and documents
• Update status through the trip: Ready to Pickup, Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered
• Compulsory live GPS tracking keeps the office and customer updated while a load is in transit
• Capture pickup and delivery proof photos, get the receiver's signature, and generate the POD automatically
• Upload the paperwork a load needs, right from your phone
• Onboard once — driver license, insurance, and signed agreements
• Get push alerts the moment a load is offered near you

Built for drivers and fleet owners who’d rather run the job than chase it.

An active Bestloaders carrier account is required to use this app.
```

## Description — Variant B (detailed)  `[~1650]`
```
Bestloaders FMS puts your whole freight workflow in your pocket. It’s the official carrier companion for fleet owners and drivers hauling Bestloaders' loads — from the first bid to the final proof of delivery.

FIND AND WIN LOADS
• Browse open loads and place bids, with live updates as bids come in
• Track everything you’ve bid on under My Bids
• Get instant push notifications when a load is offered near you — offers move fast, so you don’t miss out

RUN THE TRIP
• Open any assigned load for the full picture: route, container and chassis, pickup and drop details, rate and remarks
• Move the load forward with one tap — Ready to Pickup, Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered, and more
• Live GPS tracking is built in and required from pickup through delivery, so the office and the customer always know where the freight is

PROVE THE WORK
• Snap pickup and delivery proof photos
• Capture the receiver’s signature on your phone at the door
• Proof of Delivery is generated automatically
• Upload any other documents a load needs

GET SET UP ONCE
• Complete carrier onboarding in the app — driver license, insurance, and the agreements you sign to haul

Bestloaders FMS is a business tool for approved carriers. You’ll need an active carrier account, or you can register from the sign-in screen to get started.
```
