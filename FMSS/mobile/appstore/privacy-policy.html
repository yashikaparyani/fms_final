Bestloaders FMS (the “App”) is a business tool for freight carriers and fleet owners who work with <<PLACEHOLDER: legal entity name>> (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the App collects, why, and your choices. Questions: <<PLACEHOLDER: support@bestloaders.com>>.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and profile details, used to sign you in and identify your carrier account.
  • Precise location — GPS location, including in the background, but only while a load you have picked up is in transit. It powers live shipment tracking for the broker and customer. Tracking is not active before pickup or after delivery.
  • Photos and documents — pickup and delivery proof photos, delivery documents, and onboarding documents (e.g. driver license, insurance) that you choose to capture or upload.
  • Delivery signature — a signature captured on your device from the person receiving the freight.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used to send you load-offer and dispatch alerts.

How we use it

We use this information solely to operate the App’s features: authentication, showing and bidding on loads, live shipment tracking, proof of pickup and delivery, document handling, and notifications. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Tracking & third parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it with data brokers. Data is transmitted to our own backend to provide the service, and push notifications are delivered via Apple Push Notification service and Expo’s push service.

Data retention

We keep your account and load-related data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service and meet legal, tax, and freight-recordkeeping obligations.

Your choices & account deletion

  • You control location, camera, photo, and notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time. Declining location will prevent pickup/tracking, which the service requires.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data from within the App, or by emailing <<PLACEHOLDER: support@bestloaders.com>>. We will delete personal data except records we must retain by law.

Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.
