```
Bestloaders FMS is the carrier app for independent trucking companies and owner-operators hauling freight brokered by S Line Brokerage. Find work, run the job, and get the paperwork done — all from your phone.

FIND AND WIN LOADS
• Browse loads open for bidding and place your bid in seconds
• Watch bids update live, and track everything you have bid on under My Bids
• Get a push notification the moment a load is offered near you — offers move fast

RUN THE TRIP
• Open an assigned load for the full picture: route, container and chassis numbers, appointment windows, PO numbers, pieces, weight and your rate
• Move the load forward with one tap — Ready to Pickup, Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered, and more
• Live GPS keeps the office and the customer updated while the freight is moving, so you spend less time answering "where is it?"

PROVE THE WORK
• Capture pickup and delivery proof photos
• Take the receiver's signature on your phone at the door
• Proof of Delivery is generated automatically
• Upload any other document the load needs

GET SET UP ONCE
• Complete carrier onboarding in the app — driver licence, insurance and the agreements you sign to haul
• Keep your drivers and trucks on file

ABOUT LOCATION
This app collects location data to show the office and the customer where a load is, and to record where it was picked up and delivered — even when the app is closed or not in use. Location is shared only while a load you have picked up is in transit, and it stops at delivery. You are asked before it starts and can turn it off at any time in your device settings.

WHO IT IS FOR
Any licensed motor carrier can apply from the sign-in screen. The app is free; carriers are paid for the loads they haul and are never charged to use it.
```
