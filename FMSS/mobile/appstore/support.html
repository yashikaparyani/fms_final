Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?

You need an approved carrier account with Bestloaders. You can register from the sign-in screen in the app, or contact us to get set up.

Why does the app need my location?

Live GPS tracking is required from pickup through delivery so the office and the customer can see the freight’s position and ETA. Location is only shared while a load you’ve picked up is in transit — never before pickup or after delivery.

How do I complete onboarding?

In the app, add your driver license and insurance and sign the carrier agreements. A driver can’t report a pickup or delivery until a license is on file.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account and data deletion from within the app, or by emailing <<PLACEHOLDER: support@bestloaders.com>>. See our Privacy Policy.

A button or screen isn’t working

Make sure you’re on the latest version from the App Store, then restart the app. If it persists, email us with your device model and iOS version.
